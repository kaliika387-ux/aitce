About Aitec Primary School

Providing quality education with Islamic values since 2005.

Aitec Primary School & Quran Memorisation Centre is located on Entebbe Road, Namulanda, Kampala. We serve Muslim and non-Muslim families seeking quality education with Islamic values.

Our motto "Seek Knowledge Always" guides everything we do. From P1 to P7, we blend the national curriculum with Quranic studies to raise academically strong, God-fearing children.

Contact us today at 0702323677 & 0782489915 for school tours and 2026 admissions.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To provide excellent, affordable education that develops the mind, nurtures Islamic character, and prepares learners for life.

Vision

To be the leading primary school in Kampala producing scholars who excel in both dunya and akhirah.

Values

Discipline, Integrity, Excellence, Taqwa, Respect for knowledge and people.
